# 2026-11-12

Started the procedural-world list (terrain, mesh generator, scatter, visual editor) with the terrain generator:
`terrain/include/aether/terrain/generator.h`. Stages: domain-warped fractal plus ridged noise, thermal erosion, droplet hydraulic erosion,
depression filling (priority flood) and flow accumulation for rivers and lakes, then biomes, material weights and a splatmap.
Deterministic from the seed (no clock, no threads).

Two defects found by looking at the output, not by the first tests: droplets that died carrying sediment deleted it (the mean height fell
3.5 m per 20,000 droplets on a 60 m terrain; they set it down now, and a test holds the mean to half a percent), and filled depressions
drew as flat parallel river bars (they are lakes now, uncut). Not fixed: a few bars where two large rivers meet, because both cut their
banks into the same cells.

Not done: the other three items; an MCP tool and a `.aterrain` asset for it; feeding the result into the player's terrain renderer
(the heightmap and the splatmap are the formats it already takes); threading.
