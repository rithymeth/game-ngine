# 2026-11-15

The visual generation editor, item 4 of the procedural-world list, as editor panels (`editor/src/world/procedural_document.h`, `procedural_panel.h`).

- `ProceduralWorldDocument` (headless, like the other documents): every setting of the terrain generator, the scatter rules, and a mesh family per rule;
  four presets (temperate, alpine, desert, islands); undo that takes one step per edit and not per slider tick; seeds and a dice button; Generate; a
  preview picture; and three ways out. `ApplyToTerrain` hands the heightmap, layers and splatmap to the Terrain editor (its sculpt tools carry on),
  `ApplyToFoliage` the instances to the Foliage editor (one undo step), and `Bake` writes files under `Content/Generated/<name>/`: the terrain as a model with
  six surfaces, a `.glb` per mesh variant, and an `.ascene` with the terrain and the scatter as model entities, which the player loads.
- `ProceduralWorldPanel`: tabs for Terrain (sliders with tooltips), Scatter (rule list, per-rule editor, mesh family) and Output; regenerates as you edit when
  the last generation was quick, once the edit settles. Registered in the workspace under World.
- The terrain module is not in the player (no terrain component or asset type; the MCP terrain tools say so too), so a bake exports the terrain as a mesh. That is
  why the output is a model and not a terrain asset.

Rendering a baked world in the player found three defects in the generator that the tests had not. Hydraulic erosion made the terrain spiky: it raised the mean
|laplacian| from 0.06 m to 0.47 m, and the cause was the sediment capacity (4, the usual figure for a 0..1 height range, carries metres per droplet on a 60 m terrain);
0.5 leaves it at about 0.11 m and still cuts gullies, and the erosion now digs over a disc and not one cell. Lakes drew on their bumpy beds (a flat surface at each
lake's fill level now, and at the sea level for the sea). A 3x3 smoothing stage (`smoothing`, default 2) was added first as the suspected fix; it barely changed the
roughness (the noise was not the cause) but it is kept as a setting.

Not done: grass, shrubs, reeds and cacti have no mesh generator, so their rules are scattered and shown in the preview but baked as nothing; the terrain's biome
borders are stair-stepped (one surface per quad); the terrain edge is an open lip; a baked scene is one draw call per object (the RHI has no instancing), capped at
5,000 objects by default; no node graph; the terrain's textures are single colours.
