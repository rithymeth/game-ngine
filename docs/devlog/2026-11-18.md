# 2026-11-18

Two flaws left in the baked worlds, fixed in `ProceduralWorldDocument::BuildTexturedTerrainModel` and `RenderTerrainTexture`.

- **Shorelines.** The water was drawn per quad, so a lake's edge was a staircase of whole quads. A quad with any corner in the water now gets a flat quad at that
  water's level (a lake's fill level, or the sea's), which runs on under the dry corners; the ground hides it there, so the visible edge is where the ground
  really crosses the water level. The texture's lake and sea beds follow the same line (how far under the surface the ground is, from the highest water level
  among the four samples round a texel), so the dark bed ends where the water does.
- **The terrain's edge.** A skirt (`BakeOptions::terrain_skirt`, 6 m by default, 0 for the old open edge) carries the four edges down below the lowest ground,
  flat shaded and textured by the ground texture's edge stretched down, so the terrain is a block and not a sheet. A third primitive, using the ground's material.

Both checked in the player from two angles. The skirt's streaks are the edge texture stretched down; they read as soil strata but are not an earth texture.

Not done: an earth/strata texture for the skirt; the bottom of the block is open (nothing looks at it); only a base colour on the ground (no normal map or detail
texture); rivers are painted, so they have no surface motion or reflections; water is one flat colour with no depth tint or foam.
