# 2026-11-18

Two flaws left in the baked worlds, fixed in `ProceduralWorldDocument::BuildTexturedTerrainModel` and `RenderTerrainTexture`.

- **Shorelines.** The water was drawn per quad, so a lake's edge was a staircase of whole quads. A quad with any corner in the water now gets a flat quad at that
  water's level (a lake's fill level, or the sea's), which runs on under the dry corners; the ground hides it there, so the visible edge is where the ground
  really crosses the water level. The texture's lake and sea beds follow the same line (how far under the surface the ground is, from the highest water level
  among the four samples round a texel), so the dark bed ends where the water does.
- **The terrain's edge.** A skirt (`BakeOptions::terrain_skirt`, 6 m by default, 0 for the old open edge) carries the four edges down below the lowest ground,
  flat shaded and textured by the ground texture's edge stretched down, so the terrain is a block and not a sheet. A third primitive, using the ground's material.

Both checked in the player from two angles. The skirt's streaks are the edge texture stretched down; they read as soil strata but are not an earth texture.

Not done: an earth/strata texture for the skirt; the bottom of the block is open (nothing looks at it); only a base colour on the ground (no normal map or detail
texture); rivers are painted, so they have no surface motion or reflections; water is one flat colour with no depth tint or foam.

## Play in the editor: an error says which entity it happened on, and clicking it goes there

An error used to be a line of text in the Console, with the entity's name and the first eight characters of its GUID buried
in the sentence. That is a report, not a lead: with forty doors named "Door" in a level, "Door.luau:4" alone does not say
which one, and there was nothing to click.

The information is now data beside the text. `ScriptSystem` records an `ErrorOrigin` for every entry in `Errors()`, in the
same order: the entity's GUID, the name it had at the time, and the callback (`OnUpdate`, `OnStart`, a method called from
C++). Errors that belong to no one entity -- a class that would not load, an event handler or timer, a Blueprint -- get an
empty origin. `Game::RuntimeErrors()` pairs each text with its origin and appends the Blueprint errors (text only), and
`LiveViewport` and the Scene View's `SceneViewShading` interface carry the list to the editor. A destroy-on-error case
is handled too: an error raised while ending play for an entity already gone has no GUID to offer, and says so by having
none.

In PIE the Scene View lists the errors under its banner -- up to three rows, a red panel that clips long messages, with
"+N more -- the Messages tool lists them all" when there are more. A row that names an entity is a link: the row lights
under the mouse and a click selects the entity, so the failure and its cause are one gesture, without leaving the
viewport. The rows' rectangles are reported in `PieOverlay::errors`, so a test clicks them like a person would.

Messaging is the other half. `EditorWorkspace::Update` polls the running game each frame while the document plays and
posts the first sight of each error to the notification service as an `Error` with an `Entity` target. The key is the text
with the game's " (repeated N times)" suffix cut out and the GUID appended, so a broken `OnUpdate` that fails every frame
is one row, not a new row at each logging threshold. Messages sends its click back through the same path as every other
target: the entity is selected and the Scene tool is focused. The seen-set is cleared when the session ends, so a second
play session reports its errors again; the rows from the first stay in the history.

Tests: `SceneView_PieRuntimeErrorsLinkToTheirEntity` (rows under the banner, linked row selects, unlinked row does not,
Stop clears the panel), `EditorWorkspace_RuntimeErrorsReachMessagesWithTheEntity` (one row with the entity target, the
repeated suffix does not add rows, a new session reports again), and the origin assertions in
`ScriptSystem_ErrorsSayWhichEntityAndWhereAndDoNotFloodTheList`.
