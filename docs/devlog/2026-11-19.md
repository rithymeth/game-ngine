# 2026-11-19

The water of a baked world has a texture. `terrain::RenderWaterTexture` paints it over the whole terrain (the same layout as the ground texture, so the water's UV is the
position over the map) from the water's depth at each point, which is the lake's fill level or the sea's minus the ground's height, found by the same helper the ground
texture's lake and sea beds use (`WaterLevelAround`, now shared): turquoise in the shallows to dark blue in the deep, and a white foam band along the shore about
0.7 m wide, broken up with noise so it is a lacy edge and not a stripe. No renderer change: it is a base colour texture on the water's glTF material, with the
roughness down to 0.1 for the sky's reflection. `BakeOptions::water_texture` is 512 by default (0 for the old flat blue).

Checked in the player: the shore foams, the shallows are turquoise and the middle of the lake is dark blue. (A second scratch camera put me under a mountain, which
showed the terrain's underside open, as expected.)

Not done: the water does not move (no flow, no ripples, no normal map; a water shader would need renderer support), no transparency (so no seeing the bed through the
shallows: the depth tint stands in for it), no reflections of the terrain (only the sky's), and rivers are painted into the ground, not given this water.
