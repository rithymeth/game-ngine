# 2026-11-22

Swaying vegetation, the second thing to read the scene shader's game time.

- A material extension `AETHER_materials_wind` (`GltfMaterial::wind`, metres of sway at the top, 0 to 0.3), four bits of the draw's packed options (bits 12 to 15, 0.02 m a
  step), read in the vertex shader. The sway is two slow swells (one varying along the object a little so the blades of a tuft do not move in step), weighted by
  height above the object's base (`h / (h + 0.3)`), so the roots stay put and a tall tree's leaves move about as far as a tuft's tips.
- The phase is the instance's: push constants are full, but an opaque material never reads its colour's alpha, so for an opaque wind material the renderer packs
  the phase there (from where the instance stands in the world, 0.35 rad a metre east and 0.27 north, so the sway travels across a field like a gust and a field of
  grass does not move as one). A Mask or Blend wind material keeps its alpha and has no phase.
- To read the time the vertex shader now comes after the frame constants (it was before them); the shader source is assembled in that order.
- The generators set it: grass 0.14, reeds 0.12, shrub and tree leaves 0.08 (a `sway` setting on each, 0 for none, clamped to the 0.3 the bits can carry); bark, stems of a
  shrub, rocks and cacti do not sway. The exporter writes the extension and lists it as used beside water.

Checked: a GPU test renders a grass tuft with and without the flag at two game times a second apart: flagged moves (6.7 mean step of 255), unflagged does not (0.0000),
the same time twice is identical.

Not done: shadows do not sway (the shadow caster shaders do not have the wind, so a swaying tuft's shadow stands still: invisible for grass, a little visible for a
tree); the strength is quantised to 0.02 m and one gust shape, not a per-material wind direction or a global wind setting; instanced scenes pay the same per-draw
cost (the phase is per draw); the leaves' normals do not follow the displacement (a few centimetres); billboard or impostor LODs do not sway.
