# 2026-11-11

Screen-space ambient occlusion in the player's post pass (`player/src/post_pass.cpp`): depth-only, 12 taps, no blur, on by default
where the HDR path and the depth copy exist. The push constants grew from 32 to 64 bytes. Checked with A/B renders of the valley
(`AETHER_RENDER_AO=0` against the default); the effect is local and modest because the valley is low-poly. Not measured: GPU cost.
Not done: a blur, a lit-only term (applying it to ambient light alone would stop it dimming sunlit ground), an image test.

Measured the AO noise on open ground before building a blur: the ratio against the unoccluded frame has a standard deviation of 0.007,
below what shows, so there is no blur. Then distance fog: `PostProcessVolume::fog_density` (1/m, default 0, so no scene changes),
blended like the other settings and applied in the post pass from the same depth copy, toward the frame's sky colour; the sky pixels
are left alone. On the valley overview at 0.006 the spires and ridges recede into the haze, which was the biggest gap in the frames; that
is strong, 0.002 to 0.003 is gentler. Not done: height fog, sun in-scatter, fog on transparent draws (they never reach the depth copy).

Sun in-scatter in the fog: toward the sun the haze takes the sun's colour (`pow(dot(view ray, to-sun), 8)` times the sun colour times 0.1;
`AETHER_RENDER_FOG_SUN` tunes the 0.1). The push constants are 112 bytes now. Checked on a frame facing the sun (0.23 of a level off the
mean, 19 at most); a frame facing away from it is bit-identical, as it should be. Not done: height fog, and the sun disc itself (the
sky pass draws it, the fog does not).
