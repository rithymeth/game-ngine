# The work-stealing queue (engine/include/aether/job/work_stealing_queue.h) is a Chase-Lev deque that
# publishes a Job through standalone atomic_thread_fence calls. ThreadSanitizer does not model
# standalone fences, so it reports the plain Job fields as racing even though the fence orders them.
# Suppressed narrowly: only the two functions that touch Job fields.
race:aether::JobSystem::Execute
race:aether::JobSystem::AllocateJob
